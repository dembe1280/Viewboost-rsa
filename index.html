Promote your video properly

The legitimate way to get more viewers is a Google Ads video campaign, which shows your video to real people.

1
Create a Google Ads account

Sign in at ads.google.com with your Google account.

2
Pick a video campaign

Choose a goal such as views or awareness and the YouTube video campaign type.

3
Set audience and budget

Choose locations (for example South Africa), age, interests and a daily budget you are comfortable with.

4
Add your video

Select your YouTube video, write a clear headline and a strong first five seconds.

5
Measure and improve

Watch results in Google Ads and YouTube Studio, then adjust targeting and creative.

!
What to avoid

Bots, view farms or players that loop a video. YouTube removes those views and can penalise the channel.
